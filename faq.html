Frequently Asked Questions

1How do I know if my child is struggling with reading?
Common signs include difficulty sounding out words, slow or hesitant reading, trouble understanding stories, avoiding reading, or becoming frustrated with homework. Our free reading assessment can help you identify areas where your child may need additional support.
2Is the reading assessment really free?
Yes. The initial assessment is free and is designed to help parents better understand their child’s reading strengths and challenges.
There is no obligation to purchase a program.
3What ages is Read Like Rocky designed for?
Read Like Rocky is designed to support children at different stages of reading development. The assessment helps identify an appropriate starting point based on reading ability, rather than relying on age or grade alone.
4What happens after my child completes the assessment?
You’ll receive information about your child’s reading strengths and areas that may need improvement. You’ll also be introduced to reading lessons and activities that may be appropriate for their current skill level.
5Do I need teaching experience to help my child?
No. Read Like Rocky is designed for parents without professional teaching experience. The program includes instructions, guided activities, and resources to help you support your child’s reading development at home.
6How much time should we spend practicing?
Short, consistent practice sessions are often easier for children to manage than lengthy lessons. The appropriate amount of practice depends on your child’s age, attention span, and reading needs.
7What makes Read Like Rocky different from other reading programs?
Read Like Rocky combines structured reading activities with parent guidance and assessments intended to help families identify specific reading needs.
The program draws on the educational experience of Dr. Nancy Barker, who has spent more than 40 years working in classroom teaching, special education, school administration, curriculum development, and university education.
8Can Read Like Rocky help children with learning disabilities?
The program may provide additional reading practice for children with different learning needs. However, it does not replace specialized instruction, a professional evaluation, or services provided through an Individualized Education Program.
9Will my child be assigned lessons based on their reading level?
That’s the intended approach. Assessment results will be used to recommend lessons appropriate to the child’s demonstrated reading skills.
The placement process must be fully tested before launch.
10How quickly will my child’s reading improve?
Every child progresses differently. Improvement depends on their starting skills, learning needs, consistency of practice, and the instruction they receive.
We do not promise a specific improvement within a guaranteed timeframe.
11Can teachers and schools use Read Like Rocky?
We’re developing Read Like Rocky with opportunities for future use by educators and schools. Teachers and school administrators interested in the program can contact us to discuss potential applications.
12What if I still have questions?
We welcome questions from parents, teachers, and caregivers. Visit our Contact Us page to reach the Read Like Rocky team.
